Who we are

A movement, not a lecture.

The Climate Truth (TCT) is an international, digital-first climate advocacy platform. For decades the climate story has been told in statistics — we tell it in human stories, gathered from the people already living the consequences, and we convert them into action people can actually take this week.

We are a stage, not a hierarchy: no membership fees, no gatekeeping, and no requirement to be an expert before you contribute. Just structure, mentorship, and reach.

  • — Storytelling over statistics — emotional, plain-language narratives
  • — Accessible grassroots action designed for low-resource settings
  • — An open platform where advocates lead their own projects
  • — A special focus on Global South voices and ground truths

Our core pathways

How we turn truth into action.

These pathways are deliberately open-ended. As new advocates join, new pathways open — everything here is built to move people from understanding the crisis to doing something about it, at the lowest possible barrier to entry.

01

Storytelling

Two distinct streams: AI-assisted creative climate narratives, and firsthand grassroots testimonies from frontline communities. Both are built to be felt and shared, not skimmed.

02

Action Challenges

Structured community challenges such as Waste to Life — guided, step-by-step, achievable with materials already in the house, and shared back by the people who complete them.

03

Micro-Learning & Education

Short-form Eco-Hacks and a foundational video series: what's really happening, what climate change actually is, who is responsible, and why climate action is global justice.

04

Open Platform

Any advocate, student, researcher, or climate professional can propose and lead a project. We provide the stage, the structure, the audience, and the support.

05

Research & Collaboration

University partnerships, fellowships, and comparative climate studies that convert lived experience into open-access evidence.

Strategic roadmap

Two pillars, one research track.

Digital reach on one side, physical community action on the other, and an academic track that turns both into evidence. Everything below is either running or in active development.

Pillar I

Digital Innovation & Engagement

Reach, narrative, and micro-learning

  • AI StorytellingThe creative narrative series — Dual Timelines, Microbe History, Lost Green, Letters from Future Gen Z, Conversations with Nature.
  • Eco-Hacks Micro-LearningShort video hacks: visual waste-to-life transformations, micro-urban gardening, eco-budgeting for sustainable living, and the microbiology of daily eco-habits.
  • Global Youth FellowshipConnecting passionate young advocates with mentors worldwide.
Pillar II

Onsite Community Action

Hands, ground, and neighbourhoods

  • Urban Plantation DrivesVertical greening and micro-space planting in high-density urban areas.
  • Waste-to-Life HubsLocal upcycling and community recycling workshops that keep material out of landfill.
  • Eco-Plogging & Fitness DrivesCombining health, fitness, and environmental cleanup in one community event.
Track

Academic & Research

University collaborations

  • Global Climate FellowshipsPairing local students with international environmental professionals.
  • Comparative Climate StudiesCross-regional research on climate impact, vulnerability, and resilience.
  • Publications & AI ToolsOpen-access climate research plus practical AI tooling for climate tracking.

Open invitation

Submit your climate truth.

"Wherever you are in the world, share your region's climate truth and lived realities. We provide the stage to amplify your voice."

TCT exists to aggregate, document, and amplify localized climate realities — the ground truths that rarely reach a global audience. Submissions can be a personal account, a photo-story, a local observation, a research summary, or an idea for a project you want to lead. Nothing is published without your explicit consent, and you keep authorship of your own story.

No fee, no membership, no experience required. We reply to every submission.
